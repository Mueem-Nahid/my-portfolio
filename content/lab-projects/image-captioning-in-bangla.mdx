## What it does

Upload an image, get an AI-generated caption in Bangla (with English fallback). Includes a training pipeline so custom models can be trained on your own image/caption datasets, plus batch captioning and model management APIs.

## How it's built

Next.js 15 frontend, FastAPI ML service (TensorFlow/Keras CNN-LSTM, OpenCV/PIL preprocessing), PostgreSQL + Redis, Nginx reverse proxy — everything containerized with separate dev and production compose files.

## Current state

Early stage — local Docker stack runs; the hard part ahead is training data quality for Bangla captions, documented in the model training guide.
