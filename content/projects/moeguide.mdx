## Problem

Tourists needed a way to find, hire, and schedule local guides — and guides needed a structured way to manage bookings, payments, and conversations in one place.

## Approach

Built the platform on the T3 stack (Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, tRPC, Prisma) with an admin panel: guide hiring, calendar-style scheduling similar to Google Calendar, payment integration, and in-app user–guide messaging.

Beyond shipping features, responsible for code reviews and mentoring junior developers, with project maintenance run through Jira.

## Outcome

The platform is in production, connecting tourists and guides end to end.
