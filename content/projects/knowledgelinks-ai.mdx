## Problem

The product needed to answer questions with context drawn from users' own knowledge bases — where a single source could be a large document, an audio file, or an image.

## Approach

Engineered and scaled a Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline with LangChain and OpenAI over ChromaDB and PostgreSQL, delivering context-aware responses.

To keep responses fast under heavy inputs, large documents, audio, and images are processed asynchronously through queues and Redis, with progress pushed to the client over WebSockets.

## Outcome

The system runs in production, handling multi-modal knowledge sources at scale for the client.
