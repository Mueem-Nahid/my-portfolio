## Problem

A Japanese sanitation certification service needed to run its business on subscriptions: recurring billing, mid-cycle plan changes with correct proration, reliable invoicing, and a portal where customers manage everything themselves.

## Approach

Architected the billing system end to end on Stripe — recurring payments, webhooks, plan upgrades, proration, invoice and receipt PDF generation, retry flows for failed payments, and a self-service customer portal.

Alongside billing, built a high-throughput email campaign engine on AWS SES with per-recipient delivery tracking, processing jobs asynchronously via Upstash QStash.

## Outcome

The platform is in production today, serving the certification service's billing and customer-facing email delivery end to end.
